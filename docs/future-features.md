# Future Features

This document captures ideas that are intentionally out of scope for the current 4.x line but form the long-term vision of CubeNet AI Squad.

## Persistent AI Memory

Bots remember notable events (who killed them repeatedly, successful plays, map-specific lessons) and adjust future behaviour accordingly.

## Individual Skill Progression

Bots slowly improve (or specialise) based on performance metrics, unlocking higher skill tiers or new behavioural traits over many maps.

## Team Coordination & Squad Leadership

- Designated squad leaders that issue simple orders (push, hold, fall back).
- Role assignment (pocket, roamer, flanker, etc.) influenced by personality.
- Basic call-outs or coordinated timing.

## Dynamic Personalities

Personality values can drift over time in response to experience, or can be temporarily modified by in-game events (tilt, clutch performances, etc.).

## Advanced Voice Interaction

- Real voice lines (sound files) instead of / in addition to chat.
- Context-sensitive responses (revenge, domination, near-death, celebrating a teammate’s kill).
- Personality-specific voice packs.

## Weapon & Loadout Preferences

Each profile can express preferred weapons or loadout styles that the system tries to honour when possible.

## Adaptive Combat Learning

Simple statistical models or rule sets that let bots adapt to the current enemy team’s habits within a single map or across a session.

## Campaign / Long-Term Persistence

Bot profiles and memory survive not only map changes and restarts but can be carried across an entire “campaign” or season of server activity.

## Observability

- In-game or web-based dashboard showing live squad stats, personality values, and recent voice / combat events.
- Exportable history for community highlights or balancing decisions.

---

None of the above are promised for any particular release. They exist to keep design decisions aligned with the long-term goal of turning TF2 bots into memorable, persistent squad members.
